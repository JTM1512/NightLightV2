Crayons vs Nightmares

You are inside a kid's head, fighting off his nightmare. So the HUD shouldn't look designed at all. It should look like he made it: crayon drawings, torn notebook paper, gold star stickers and sticky tape. The nightmare is the opposite: wet black ink, dripping red letters and scribbles that don't stay still. The two fight over the screen. When the kid's sanity is high, his drawings fill the HUD. As the Dream Core gets hurt, the ink crawls in from the edges, scribbles over his drawings and starts to break the text.

1. Two sides fighting over the screen

The kid (sanity)
  • Crayon, coloured pencil, wobbly lines
  • Torn paper scraps, lined notebook pages, tape
  • Warm, bright colours: yellow, orange, sky blue, green
  • Handwritten font, a bit messy, with spelling-test energy
  • Gold star stickers are the tokens. Stick figures are the defender slots.
  • Defenders are his drawings: a paper plane, a slingshot, a torch
The nightmare
  • Wet black ink, splatters and frantic scribbles
  • Dripping red lettering that jitters
  • Almost no colour: black, blood red, a sickly green
  • Glitched, broken letters once it is winning
  • Enemies are scribbled black shapes with red eyes
  • It never gets a panel of its own. It eats the kid's panels instead.

Sanity drives the whole HUD

Sanity is the Dream Core's health. The HUD reads it every time OnCoreHealthChanged fires and moves between three states:

SanityStateWhat the HUD does
100 to 60%BraveClean crayon drawings, bright colours, the sun face in the sanity bar is smiling. Ink only lurks in the far corners.
60 to 30%ScaredInk creeps in from the edges and scribbles over the corners of paper scraps. The sun face looks worried. Warning text drips. Lines start to "boil" (wobble) a little faster.
Below 30%Losing itInk covers the screen edges and tears the panels. Crayon colours drain to grey. Labels glitch and misspell themselves ("Sh00ter"). A slow heartbeat pulse. The nightmare's eyes open in the ink.

5. Building it in Unreal

Make the art by hand

  • Draw the icons, panel scraps and lamp with real crayons on paper, then scan or photograph them. It is quick and it can't look like any other game.
  • Tear real paper for the scraps and scan those too, with a transparent background.
  • Ink splats and scribbles: draw with a marker or ink pen, scan, and make them pure black with alpha.

Line boil

  • Draw (or slightly trace) each icon three times and cycle the frames at about 8 fps. The wobble makes it feel hand drawn and alive.
  • Cheaper option: a UI material that offsets UVs with a slow noise texture.
  • Boil faster as sanity drops, so the HUD itself looks nervous.

The ink overlay

  • One full screen Image with a UI material: a scanned ink texture masked by a noise texture compared against a Sanity parameter. Lower sanity means more of the mask passes, so the ink grows inward from the edges.
  • Add a panner to the noise so the ink edge crawls slowly.
  • Below 30%, fade in two red eye sprites inside the ink and drop the saturation of the HUD widgets.

Widgets and order

  • 1. WBP_SanityBar and the ink overlay (they carry the whole idea).
  • 2. WBP_StarCount and WBP_StickFigures.
  • 3. WBP_LampButton with StartNextWaveNow.
  • 4. WBP_DrawingShelf with a WBP_DrawingCard per defender.
  • 5. WBP_NotebookPage for upgrades (defenders and the Core).
  • 6. WBP_NightmareText: dripping warnings, and the glitch font swap below 30%.
